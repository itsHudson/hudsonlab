Professional Experience

Key contributions across business operations

During my time with Australian Cake House / ACake (M) Sdn Bhd, I had the opportunity to contribute to different parts of the business beyond daily store execution. My involvement gradually expanded into support areas such as bookkeeping assistance, staff onboarding, SOP development, internal documentation, customer communication, basic marketing support, and outlet setup coordination.

This experience was valuable because it showed me how operational effectiveness is not built from one department alone. It depends on accurate records, clear processes, reliable communication, staff readiness, consistent service standards, and practical systems that support daily work.

Across these responsibilities, I learned how structured thinking, adaptability, and teamwork contribute to smoother operations and better business continuity.
