Experience Frame

Where practical business understanding became layered, not linear

During my time with Australian Cake House / ACake (M) Sdn Bhd, I had the opportunity to be involved in different operational and support functions rather than being limited to one fixed area. My contributions gradually expanded into bookkeeping support, recruitment coordination, onboarding assistance, SOP development, internal documentation, customer handling, training support, system setup assistance, and outlet preparation.

That range mattered because it showed me that business performance is not driven by one task alone. It depends on how records, people, workflows, communication, service, and systems reinforce one another in day-to-day operations.

Overall Reflection

Less about title, more about range

Rather than focusing on a single function, I gradually learned how different components — people, processes, systems, and customers — work together inside a real operational environment.

Core outcome This journey helped build structured thinking, adaptability, continuous learning, and stronger awareness of consistency, teamwork, and operational discipline.

Why It Matters

The foundation behind later growth

Before business systems, data, or technology thinking became formal, this phase had already introduced me to how organisations work under real constraints, real timing, and real service expectations.

Contribution Radar

Ten experience modules — designed like signals, not paragraphs

01

Accounting & Financial Support

Assisted in daily bookkeeping and financial record management, supported sales tracking and basic reporting processes, gained hands-on exposure to multi-outlet financial operations, and learned the importance of accuracy and consistency in transaction handling.

02

Human Resources & Team Support

Participated in recruitment and interview coordination, contributed to interview and onboarding improvements, assisted in training new staff, and learned how people management aligns with operational needs.

03

Operations & SOP Development

Contributed to creating and refining SOPs for daily operations, order processing, pricing structure, and customer service handling while learning the value of process consistency and scalability.

04

Information Guide & Internal Documentation

Assisted in developing Information Guide SOP content covering company background, internal structure, communication channels, and fraud awareness guidelines.

05

Training & Knowledge Sharing

Supported training in customer service standards, product knowledge, and POS system usage for better staff readiness.

06

Customer Experience & Communication

Managed interactions through WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger while assisting with orders, inquiries, concerns, and complaints.

07

Marketing & Customer Engagement

Supported basic marketing and engagement efforts, contributed ideas to improve customer reach and retention, and learned how operations affect customer growth.

08

System Implementation & Technical Support

Assisted in POS setup and configuration, supported troubleshooting of store-level technical issues, and gained exposure to system standardisation across outlets.

09

Outlet Setup & Expansion Support

Participated in new outlet setup and preparation, including staff readiness, system setup, and operational flow preparation. Supported multiple outlet openings, including shopping mall locations, and learned how launch readiness affects operational smoothness.

10

Operational Problem-Solving

Assisted in handling real-world situations such as customer complaints, equipment-related issues, and unexpected store incidents by following structured response procedures and developing a calm, solution-focused mindset under pressure.

What This Experience Strengthened

Capabilities formed through real operational involvement

  • Structured thinking across people, process, systems, and service interactions.
  • Administrative and operational support awareness in a multi-function environment.
  • Better understanding of SOP design, workflow documentation, and process consistency.
  • Professional communication across internal coordination and customer-facing channels.
  • Practical team support awareness through recruitment, onboarding, and training exposure.
  • Calm problem-solving and adaptability inside fast-moving business situations.

Overall Reflection

A business foundation built before the next chapter

Throughout this experience, I was involved in various aspects of operations, from administrative support to store-level execution and broader coordination work.

Instead of learning through one function alone, I gradually understood how people, processes, systems, and customers interact in a real business environment.

This journey gave me a strong foundation in structured thinking, adaptability, and continuous learning, while helping me understand why consistency, teamwork, and clear operational systems matter so much.
