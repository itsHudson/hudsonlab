Professional Experience

Where business understanding became practical

During my time with Australian Cake House / ACake (M) Sdn Bhd, I was given the opportunity to contribute across different layers of the business rather than staying within one narrow function. My involvement included support for bookkeeping, recruitment coordination, SOP documentation, internal guides, customer communication, staff training, system setup support, and outlet preparation.

This made the experience especially meaningful because it showed me that smooth operations depend on much more than daily execution alone. They depend on accurate records, clear workflows, reliable communication, staff readiness, service consistency, and structured systems.

The result was not only broader exposure, but also a stronger mindset around process clarity, operational discipline, and learning how different parts of a business must work together.
